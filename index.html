How I work

Problem first. Tool second.

AI helps me move quickly, but it is not the starting point. The real work is finding the bottleneck, deciding what matters and reducing the solution to something people will actually use.

1

Find the real bottleneck

Separate the visible annoyance from the underlying commercial problem.

2

Remove unnecessary complexity

Decide what the solution must do — and what it can safely ignore.

3

Build the smallest useful version

Turn the idea into something visible and testable before it becomes a large project.

4

Test the impact

Does it save time, reveal an opportunity or help someone make a better decision?

What interests me
